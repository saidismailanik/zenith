## ✨ Features

### 🎥 Instant Streaming
Browse a constantly updated library of movies and tap **▶ Play** to start streaming immediately. No sign-up or login needed.

### 🔍 Movie Details
Every title comes with a full detail page including:
- Backdrop and poster art
- Rating, release year, and runtime
- Overview / synopsis
- Full cast list
- Genre tags
- Similar movie recommendations

### 📥 Offline Downloads
Tap **📥 Download** on any available movie to save it to your device. Watch it later without an internet connection. Downloaded titles are marked **✓ Saved** and tracked in the **Downloads** tab.

### ⏯ Continue Watching
Zenith remembers exactly where you left off. Jump back into any movie right where you paused — your watch history is always one tap away from the home screen.

### 🔤 Subtitles
Subtitle support is built in. Zenith automatically fetches subtitles for supported titles so you can watch in any language or with captions on.

### 🏠 Home Feed
The home screen surfaces:
- **Trending** movies updated regularly
- **Genre browsing** — filter by action, drama, comedy, and more
- **Continue Watching** row for your in-progress titles

### 🔕 Ad-Free / Premium Mode
Zenith is free and ad-supported. To go ad-free:
- **Watch a short rewarded video** in Settings to unlock **2 hours** of uninterrupted viewing.
- **Unlock permanently** with a one-time premium key — no ads, ever.

### 🔄 Auto-Updates
Zenith checks for new versions in the background and notifies you when an update is available, so you're always on the latest build.

## 🕹 How to Use

### Browsing & Playing
1. Open the app — the **Home** tab loads automatically with trending titles.
2. Scroll or filter by genre to find something to watch.
3. Tap a movie poster to open its detail page.
4. Tap **▶ Play** to start streaming.

### Downloading for Offline
1. Open a movie's detail page.
2. Tap **📥 Download** — the button will show download progress as a percentage.
3. Once complete it shows **✓ Saved**. Find all your downloads in the **Downloads** tab.

### Resuming a Movie
From the home screen, find the **Continue Watching** row and tap the title to pick up where you left off.

### Going Ad-Free
1. Go to the **Settings** tab.
2. Tap **Watch a video to go ad-free** and complete the short rewarded video.
3. You'll get **2 hours** of ad-free streaming. Repeat anytime to extend it.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Stream not found" on Play | The stream may be temporarily unavailable. Try again in a few minutes. |
| Download stuck at a percentage | Check your storage space and internet connection, then try again. |
| App won't install | Make sure *Install unknown apps* is enabled for your browser/file manager in Android settings. |
| Subtitles not showing | Not all titles have subtitles available. Check the player settings menu during playback. |
| Ad video won't load | Ad inventory can be low at certain times. Wait a moment and try again. |
